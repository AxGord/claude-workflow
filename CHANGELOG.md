# Changelog

## Unreleased

- engine (ENG-BATCH-TRANSITION-LIMIT): a long batch campaign hit `Max transitions (1000) reached` mid-dispatch and even `stop` was refused. Bundled `batch` now has `max_transitions: 100000`. A move that only closes the run is exempt from the cap everywhere (agent transition, skill-gate auto-move, action route): its target is terminal, or no route leads back to it and one leads on to a terminal (`isClosingMove`, `src/stations.ts`), so `stop` -> `report` -> `finish` works at the cap while a loop move is still refused. Within 5% of a workflow's cap the status / transition reply carries `WARNING: N of M transitions used ...` (`capWarning`) naming what to do. Pinned in `src/engine.test.ts`.
- engine (ENG-BATCH-TASK-READONLY-SPAWNS): bundled `batch-task`'s `review` spawns its reviewer as `workflow:reviewer` (Agent `subagent_type`), the read-only type; batch-task has no recon spawn. That type has no workflow tools, so the reviewer follows no `file-review` session any more (no `start`, no `review_session`, no `link_agent` for it) and ends with a `SubagentHandback` call carrying the `VERDICT` line. Its Write tool is limited to scratch space, and the log dir `<worktree>.log` sits beside the repo, so the reviewer's `review.md` moves to `/tmp/wf-batch-logs/<ID>/review.md` (the fix round, the decider and the `lessons` copy read it there; a re-spawned driver at `review` has no current reviewer and always spawns a fresh one, whatever review.md holds; `report.md` / `handoff.md` stay in `<worktree>.log`). Pinned in `src/batch-template.test.ts` (the spawn, and the review path through `writeToolReason`) and `src/driver.test.ts`. A heir of `batch-task` that overrides `review` switches by its owner.
- engine (ENG-READONLY-GATE-TAILS): two tails of the read-only gate. (1) The rewrite's `permissionDecision: allow` no longer refuses a grep / rg filter behind a pipe because of a flag's value: `hxq lint a.hx | grep -C 3 x` (also `-A`, `-B`, `-m`, `-e`, `rg -g`, `rg --context N`…; not `grep -d` / `--directories` or grep's `--context`, which can read files) is judged by the words that are not a flag's value (`searcherFileOperands`, per tool: grep and rg differ in which letters take a value), and `-e` / `--regexp` supply the pattern, so a file operand is still found (`grep -C 3 x f`, `grep -T x f` stay refused). (2) Two forms that passed are refused: nested braces (`{{},-i}f`: `matchingBrace` counts nesting, but not escaped or quoted braces) and a redirect whose descriptor word is not whole (bare `>&1foo` writes the file `1foo`; `2>&1foo` and `>&-foo` write none in bash but are refused as well; `>&2`, `>&-`, `>&2-` stay duplicates). README, the `workflow-authoring` skill and the pins in `src/read-only-gate.test.ts` say so.
- engine (ENG-DASH-COST): a Cost tab (`/cost`): per finished task of a day range (today by default, `from` / `to` date inputs) its outcome (landed = completed through a `land` state, `land_failed` not counting / closed = completed without landing / failed / unknown = completed with no visits recorded), review rounds, tokens and USD, and the stages-as-run each with its model, tokens and USD; the range's totals and $ per landed; a bar trend of $ per landed over the last 14 days; a note with the price table and the date it was updated. `GET /api/cost?from=&to=` returns `buildCost` (`src/calibration.ts`). Prices are new in the engine: `PRICES_USD_PER_MTOK` (blended USD per million tokens by model family) with `PRICES_UPDATED` in `src/calibration.ts`, the one owner; a model matching no family counts tokens at no price. Only ledger lines with a `task` are pipelines. Tokens show per key: `tokens_<stage>` at that stage's model, `tokens_worker` as one unsplit row at the task's model, the rest of the total as a `-` row; a line with no tokens is unmeasured (`n/a`) and stays out of the USD-per-landed denominator. Pins: `src/calibration.test.ts`, `src/dashboard.test.ts`, `src/dashboard-style.test.ts`, `src/dashboard-routes.test.ts`.
- engine (ENG-DASH-POOL): a Pool tab (`/pool`): budget, held, load, external, held locks, the pool-wide STARVED line, each lease (pid, threads/locks, priority, time held, project, session linked to `/session/<id>`, step, label, OVERRUN plaque) and each waiter in admission order with its wait and STARVED plaque, refreshed every 2 s. `GET /api/pool` returns `poolSnapshot(await pool.measuredStatus())`, the data `wf-run --status` / `pool_status` print, through the pool module (`poolSnapshot` in `src/pool.ts`; `buildApp` takes an optional `Pool`). Pins: `src/pool.test.ts`, `src/dashboard.test.ts`, `src/dashboard-style.test.ts`, `src/dashboard-routes.test.ts`.
- engine (ENG-TIMELINE-LEDGER-SEGMENTS): a ledger line records `segs`, the session's state stretches (`{s, a, b, w?}`, epoch ms, `w` = pool waits flat), and the Timeline tab draws a pruned session from them (children with their own lines as sub-rows); a line from before stays one undivided bar. Readers tolerate the field (`parseLedger` takes any JSON line; no reader lists keys). Pins: `src/timeline.test.ts`.
- engine (ENG-DASH-TIMELINE): a Timeline tab (`/timeline`, `/timeline/<YYYY-MM-DD>`): one bar per task pipeline of the day, segments by state from the session's history and its child sessions' (sub-rows), coloured by stage group (recon / plan / work / gate / review / land / wait: a name match, `work` by default, `$STATE_DIR/timeline-groups.conf` overrides a state), pool-lease waits hatched, hover = state, start, duration, an hour axis. `GET /api/timeline?day=&root=` (`buildTimeline` in `src/timeline.ts`) reads the session files and the ledger (`Storage.readLedger`; `parseLedger` moved to `src/ledger.ts`); a pruned session is one undivided ledger bar. The Plan tab's `overlaps X` reason adds `(ready, admitted ahead in the same pass)` when X is a ready line, not a running one. Pins: `src/timeline.test.ts` (push `to`, segments, waits, ledger, API), `src/engine.test.ts` (push entry), `src/dashboard-style.test.ts` (timeline render, plan wording), `src/dashboard-routes.test.ts`.

- engine (ENG-MAP-BAND-TAILS): three tails of the map's bands. Sessions in git worktrees of one repo are one band: `projectNameOf` (`src/pool.ts`, on `projectRoot`, the queue's own resolution) names the project, stored in the session context at start (`context.project`, so a removed worktree keeps its checkout's name), for the event log's `session_start` and for `/api/sessions` (a `project` field `seedEvents` reads), so `wf-SLICE` worktrees no longer split a workflow into one band each. - engine (ENG-MAP-ID-PIN): the `/map/<session id>` path of the scroll work has a pin, and F5 on the map no longer lands on another lane. A reload returned the reader by pixel offset into a first scene drawn from the session files, which is shorter than the one the event log completes (landed lanes of pruned sessions come on top), so the page clamped the offset and the reader landed on a different lane. The map now saves the top lane's key and its offset with the position (`noteScroll` in `dashboard/stable.js`, merged into the `pagehide` record) and puts that lane back from the first scene on, again for each scene until the event log is in; a wheel, touch, key or click of the reader ends it. Found by the new pin: the glide to a `/map/<id>` lane was cut short by the next scene (the status line's "live" text shifted everything by one line, and the anchor hold's instant scroll cancelled the smooth one), so the lane ended off-centre; `.map-status` now keeps its line height and a scene that lands during the glide re-aims it. The glide's window (one second) opens only at its start, a re-aim does not renew it, and the reader's wheel, touch, key or click ends it. Pins (`src/dashboard-browser.test.ts`, opt-in by `CHROME_BIN`): F5 returns to the same lane when the log makes the scene taller, and to a lane only the log has; `/map/<id>` with log-only lanes above the target ends centred; a stream of events never glides a `/map/<id>` reader back to the lane, and a wheel before the log lands leaves the view where the reader put it; `/map/<id>` opens with the lane centred and highlighted, a reader at the bottom is not carried to a new lane, the pill counts the event and returns to the plain map, F5 keeps the reader's place and does not scroll to the lane again.

- engine (ENG-QUEUE-UNKNOWN-KEYS): a task key this server does not know (written by newer code, e.g. `priority:` for an older server) no longer makes the whole queue unreadable: it is kept on write, inert for the pick, and named in `queue_next` / `queue_mark` replies (`unknown key priority on X: ignored by this server`). `queue_add` / `queue_edit` still refuse an unknown key in their own input. - engine (ENG-11): the pool no longer starves silently. (1) A lease's process tree is its own load: each pass reads the process table (`ps`, at most once a second, outside the pool lock; a failed read backs off 30 s) and sums the busy cpus of a lease's recorded group and its descendants (another lease's group excluded); `external = max(0, load − Σ max(threads, tree) − load_threshold)` over the leases with threads (a lock-only lease's tree stays external) — a lease of 4 whose command ran ~12 busy `haxe --wait` read as 7.8 cores of foreign load, and 7 waiters waited 2+ hours. An over-running lease is therefore no longer bounded by admission (only `held ≤ budget` is): it is flagged, not throttled — a tree at least `max(2, threads/2)` cpus over its threads is flagged: `holds 4 threads, runs ~12 (OVERRUN)` in `--status` / `pool_status`, `label (4, runs ~12)` in a waiting `wf-run`'s progress line, one line in the new `<pool dir>/pool.log`. (2) Starvation: `config.json` `starve_after` (seconds, default 900); a waiter queued that long is marked `STARVED` (status row, `wf-run`'s progress line, one `pool.log` line) and a pool with waiters that granted nothing that long prints `STARVED: nothing granted for …, N waiting (held, external, over-running leases)`; `pool.json` keeps `last_grant` while waiters exist. An exec waiting on a lease checks every 10 s and, once starved, records `pool_starved` on its session until no wait of the step is starved (a fanout: no shard), cleared again when a step starts and ends so a crashed server's mark does not linger: the progress heartbeat says `— STARVED: <why>`, `sessions` marks it, and `status` / `transition` of the session or any ancestor carry a `POOL STARVED: session …` line. (3) Orphaned leases: a `wf-run` lease owns its group (`owns_group`); a pass that finds the `wf-run` dead while its detached child group runs SIGTERMs the group, SIGKILLs it after 3 s, and frees the lease once it is gone — a SIGKILLed `wf-run` leaves no live child and no lease (before: the child ran on, holding the lease). The lease records the boot (`boot_ms`) and the group leader's start time (`pgid_start`) at attach; a lease from another boot or whose pgid leads another process now is dropped without a signal, and a group from another boot no longer keeps any lease alive.
